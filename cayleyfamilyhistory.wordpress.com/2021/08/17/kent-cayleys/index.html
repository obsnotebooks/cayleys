Kent Cayleys

I have now been able to link together two family lines of Cayleys originating in Kent: the Cayley-Delamere line and the Cayley-Pay line. I have also added some more family members. You can see the results at https://cayleyfamilyhistory.wordpress.com/other-families/cayley-delamere-line/.
