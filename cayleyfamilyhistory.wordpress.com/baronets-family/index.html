Baronets’ family

For a searchable graphical tree of the Cayleys connected with the Brompton, Yorkhire line, please go to http://trees.ancestry.co.uk/tree/36924306/family

  • Early Cayleys
  • To the 17th Century
  • Some 17th Century Cayleys – includes the first Cayley baronet
  • Cornelius Cayley line – descendants of Cornelius Cayley (1644-1734), barrister and 4th son of the first baronet
  • 2nd Baronet’s descendants – descendants of Sir William Cayley (d.1708)
  • 5th Baronet’s descendants – descendants of Sir Thomas Cayley (1732-92)
  • 7th Baronet’s descendants – descendants of Sir Digby Cayley (1807-83)
  • Russia merchant line – descendants of John Cayley (1730-95), British consul in St Petersburg
  • Low Hall Cayleys – descendants of Arthur Cayley (1639-1707 or 1709), 3rd son of the first baronet
  • A Canadian politician’s family – descendants of William Cayley (1807-90)
  • A banker’s family – descendants of Edward Cayley (1782-1868), a banker of Stamford, Lincolnshire
